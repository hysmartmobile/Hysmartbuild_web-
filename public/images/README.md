# HySmart Build entry-screen imagery

These three custom AI-generated construction illustrations are bundled locally and optimized as WebP files:

- `hysmart-onboarding.webp` — site team and active construction project, used for company onboarding.
- `hysmart-login.webp` — high-rise construction at dusk, used for sign-in and recovery/invitation modes.
- `hysmart-signup.webp` — construction professionals collaborating over plans, used for account sign-up.

They are product UI illustrations, not photographs of a specific real construction company or a claim of endorsement by a real company. They are stored locally so entry screens do not depend on third-party image hosts. The service worker precaches these public assets for offline shell presentation.
