# HySmart Build V1.8.0 — Integrated Construction ERP

V1.8 connects the construction commercial and operational chain:

**Estimate / BOQ → Approved Budget Baseline → Procurement Commitments → Site Progress → Variations → IPC / Client Valuation → Client Invoice → Payment / Retention → Accounting → Project Profitability → Closeout / DLP**

Included domains:
- BOQ / estimating and versioned estimates
- approved budget baselines
- progress measurement and work certificates
- IPC / client valuation and automatic retention transactions
- IPC → accounts receivable invoice integration
- project profitability / forecast final cost
- subcontractors, contracts, valuations, payments and variations
- quality inspections, check items, NCRs and quality records
- safety incidents, toolbox talks, inspections, permits and corrective actions
- equipment register, allocations, operating logs, fuel and maintenance
- document revisions and transmittals
- RFIs and technical responses
- closeout items and defects liability period defects
- executive portfolio command center
